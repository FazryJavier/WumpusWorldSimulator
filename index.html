      How to play

      Explore a dark cave grid. You only know what you sense on your current cell.

      • Breeze: a pit is in a neighbouring cell. Falling in ends the game.
      • Stench: a living Wumpus is next to you. Shoot it with an arrow.
      • Glitter: gold is on this cell. Grab it!
      • Scream: your arrow killed a Wumpus.

      Easy: grab the gold, return to (0,0) and climb out. Normal and Hard: collect all the gold.

      Switch to AI Agent mode to watch a logic agent explore and explain its reasoning.
